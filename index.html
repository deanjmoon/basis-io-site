Who built it

Built by an adviser who outsourced his own paraplanning

[basis-io] started on the adviser's side of the desk. Our founder, Damien Winberg, ran a lean advice practice with no onshore staff and outsourced all of his paraplanning and admin. He knows what a good Statement of Advice looks like when it arrives, and what it costs an adviser when one comes back wrong.

We built [basis-io] so advisers can spend their time with clients, confident that the work behind the advice is done properly.

What we do

01

Advice construction

A trained team builds the advice document from the file up - what the client said they needed, the strategy chosen, and the reasoning that connects the two. The thinking is set out on the file, so the adviser can defend the recommendation months later and the licensee can follow it without a phone call.

Admin services
