In detail

Service 01

Advice construction

We construct your advice recommendations to meet your practice standards and your licensee's compliance policies.

  • Statement of Advice production, end to end.
  • Records of Advice and supporting document review.
  • Strategy confirmation before the document is drafted.
  • Prepared to your licensee's advice policy, not a house template.

Service 02

Admin services

We handle the administrative back office workflows so that you can focus on revenue generating and client work.

  • Client data collection and file preparation.
  • Implementation and application paperwork.
  • Review pack preparation ahead of client meetings.
  • Advice implementation and compliance support.

Service 03

Practice consulting

We spend time with you and your staff to understand your workflow. Through a consultative approach, we identify the bottlenecks and the opportunities to optimise.

  • Workflow and advice process review.
  • Outsourcing design - per plan, dedicated resource, or a mix.
  • Document and template rationalisation.
  • Centralised paraplanning support for licensee groups.
