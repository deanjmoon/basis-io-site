Tab 01 · Weinberg's sales story

Sales story

Weinberg's order is fixed: the client's issues first, then what we offer, then why we are different. Most write it the other way round. Every claim must hold for what a buyer can buy today.

1.1

Positioning

Answer as [basis-io], the agentic advice construction solution. The platform is not live yet.

Say it the way you would to a licensee's head of advice. No adjectives.

Which position should lead?

The site holds the last three at once today. The team decides which one leads; the others support it.

What does the buyer hear in it that the others miss?

Describe the mechanism in plain words: what the software does, what a person still checks, what lands on the file.

Lift them from calls, file reviews, licensee audit findings and ASIC reports. Buyers trust their own vocabulary.

The site already bans seamless, revolutionary and game-changing. Add the rest.

1.2

Client issues

Weinberg's core: pains removed, problems solved, results gained. Write each from the buyer's chair. Licensees and advice practices buy for different reasons, so each gets its own column.

Pains we remove

What keeps them up at night? E.g. failed file reviews, inconsistent SOAs across authorised representatives, remediation exposure, SOA turnaround, paraplanner turnover.

Problems we solve

Concrete, observable problems. E.g. best-interests reasoning missing from the file; SOAs bouncing between adviser and compliance.

Results and opportunities we create

What changes for them? E.g. more clients per adviser, audit-ready files, faster onboarding of new advisers.

The three issues they already feel without being told

Rank them. These lead every call, email and post.

1.3

Offerings

Only after the issues. Keep it short: what is bought, how it is delivered, how it is priced.

Services that exist and can be delivered next week.

Separate what is built, what is in pilot and what is planned. Licensees will diligence this.

Per SOA, subscription, licensee panel arrangement, pilot? Who pays: the licensee or the practice?
